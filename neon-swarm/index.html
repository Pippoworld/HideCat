HOW TO PLAY

  • Move with WASD / Arrow keys, or drag anywhere on touch.
  • Your weapons fire automatically — focus on dodging.
  • Kill enemies to drop XP. Collect it to level up.
  • On level up, choose an upgrade to build your run.
  • Grab ◈ coins to spend on permanent meta upgrades.
  • Survive as long as you can. The swarm never stops.
  • Desktop: ESC/P to pause · 1·2·3 to pick an upgrade · R to reroll.
